    Common questions

    How do I find the best mandi to sell my crop?

    Use the Where to sell tool: choose your crop, quantity and location. It ranks mandis by the money you keep after transport.

    Where do these prices come from?

    Prices come from the Government of India open data portal (data.gov.in), which publishes Agmarknet mandi prices. Each price shows its date.
