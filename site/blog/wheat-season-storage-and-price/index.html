Wheat: Season, Storage and What Moves the Price

Wheat is India’s main Rabi grain. A little care in storage can protect both quality and price.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Published 27 Nov 2026

Season

Wheat is a Rabi crop. It is mostly sown from October to December and harvested around March to April. Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab, Haryana and Rajasthan are among the major growing states.

Storage

  • Dry the grain well before storing. Safe storage usually needs low moisture, around 12% or less.
  • Use clean, dry, pest-free bags or bins and keep them off the floor.
  • Check the stock now and then for insects and damp.

What moves the price

  • Arrivals after harvest, which are heavy in the spring.
  • Government procurement and the Minimum Support Price (MSP).
  • Demand from flour mills and traders.
  • Trade and stock policies announced by the government.

A quick checklist

  • Dry the grain before storage.
  • Clean and dry the store, and keep stock off the floor.
  • Check the MSP and your nearest procurement centre.
  • Compare mandi prices before you sell.

Common mistakes

  • Storing wheat that still holds moisture.
  • Waiting for a price rise without checking storage losses.
  • Selling without comparing nearby mandis.

Before you sell

This is general guidance. Sowing and harvest times differ by region and year, so check with your state agriculture department or your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

When is wheat harvested?

Mostly from about March to April, depending on the region.

What moisture is safe for storing wheat?

Low moisture, usually around 12% or less. Ask your local agriculture office for the advice that fits your area.
