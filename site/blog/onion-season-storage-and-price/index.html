Onion: Seasons, Storage and Why Prices Swing

Onion prices can change quickly. Understanding the seasons and storage helps you plan your sale.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Published 27 Nov 2026

Seasons

Onion is grown in more than one season: Kharif, late Kharif and Rabi. Maharashtra, especially the Nashik and Lasalgaon area, is a leading onion region. Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka and Gujarat also grow a lot of onion.

Curing and storage

  • Let the harvested bulbs dry and cure before storage so the skin hardens.
  • Store in a well-ventilated, dry place. Moisture and poor air flow cause rot.
  • Remove damaged or sprouting bulbs early.

Rabi onion usually keeps longer than Kharif onion.

Why the price swings

  • Heavy rain can damage crop and reduce arrivals.
  • Stock held in storage can be released or run low.
  • Export and trade policy can change demand.

A quick checklist

  • Cure the bulbs before storage.
  • Keep the store dry and airy.
  • Check the stock often and remove bad bulbs.
  • Compare mandi prices and transport cost before selling.

Common mistakes

  • Storing onions while they are still wet.
  • Packing storage too tightly so air cannot move.
  • Holding stock too long after a good price has come.

Before you sell

This is general guidance. Sowing and harvest times differ by region and year, so check with your state agriculture department or your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Which onion stores longer?

Rabi onion usually stores longer than Kharif onion.

Why did onion prices jump?

Common reasons are weather damage, low arrivals, low stocks or a change in trade policy.
