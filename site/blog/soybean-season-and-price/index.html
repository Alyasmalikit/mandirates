Soybean: Season, Moisture and Price Drivers

Soybean is a major Kharif oilseed. Dry, clean produce sells better.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Published 27 Nov 2026

Season

Soybean is a Kharif crop. It is sown with the monsoon, around June to July, and harvested around September to October. Madhya Pradesh is the leading soybean state, followed by Maharashtra and Rajasthan.

Moisture and quality

Buyers prefer dry, clean produce. High moisture can lower the price and cause damage in storage, so dry the harvest well before selling or storing.

What moves the price

  • Demand from crushers for oil and meal.
  • Prices of edible oils, including imports.
  • Arrivals after harvest.
  • Government support such as MSP.

A quick checklist

  • Dry the harvest well.
  • Clean the produce before sale.
  • Compare mandi prices and the MSP.
  • Keep the produce dry in storage.

Common mistakes

  • Selling or storing damp soybean.
  • Ignoring cleanliness, which can lower the rate.
  • Not comparing nearby mandis.

Before you sell

This is general guidance. Sowing and harvest times differ by region and year, so check with your state agriculture department or your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

When is soybean harvested?

Mostly around September to October.
