Potato: Cold Storage and Price Timing

Much of India’s potato is grown in one season and stored in cold storage, so storage decisions affect your income.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Published 27 Nov 2026

Season

In the plains, potato is mostly a Rabi crop. It is sown around October to November and dug around January to March. Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal and Bihar are among the major potato states.

Cold storage

Cold stores hold potato for months. This spreads sales across the year. When a lot of stored potato is released together, prices can fall, and when stocks run low, prices can rise.

Think before you store

  • Compare the storage rent and handling cost with the price gain you hope for.
  • Remember that the future price is not guaranteed.
  • Store only good-quality, undamaged potato.

A quick checklist

  • Ask the cold store about rent and rules.
  • Store only sound, undamaged potato.
  • Watch the price trend before you decide to release stock.
  • Keep your storage receipts safe.

Common mistakes

  • Storing without counting the rent against the possible gain.
  • Storing poor-quality potato that spoils.
  • Expecting a price rise that never comes.

Before you decide

This is general guidance. Sowing and harvest times differ by region and year, so check with your state agriculture department or your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Why store potato in a cold store?

To hold it for months and sell later, but you pay rent and the later price is not guaranteed.
