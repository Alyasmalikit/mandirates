When to Sell Your Crop: Price Trends, Storage and Timing

Timing can change your income, but nobody can predict prices perfectly. These checks help you decide with care.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

Check the trend

Open Tools and look at the trend for your crop and mandi. If today’s price is well above its recent average, selling may be attractive. If it is well below, waiting may help if you can store safely.

Check your storage

Waiting only makes sense if your produce can be stored without loss. Vegetables and fruits spoil fast. Grains can usually wait longer if kept dry and safe.

Check your needs

If you need cash for loan repayment or the next sowing, a fair price now can be better than a risky wait.

Remember

Trend signals and calculators are simple aids, not financial or trading advice. Prices can rise or fall for many reasons.

Frequently asked questions

Can MandiRates tell me when prices will rise?

No. It shows trends from past prices. It does not predict the future.
