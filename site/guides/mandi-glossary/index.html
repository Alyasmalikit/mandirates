Mandi Glossary: APMC, Quintal, Modal Price and More

Short, plain meanings of the words you see on mandi price lists.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

Common words

  • APMC: Agricultural Produce Market Committee, which runs a regulated mandi.
  • Mandi: a wholesale market where farmers and traders buy and sell produce.
  • Quintal: 100 kg.
  • Modal price: the rate at which most of the produce sold.
  • Arrivals: the amount of produce that came to the mandi.
  • Variety: the type of the crop, such as Sharbati wheat or Basmati rice.
  • Grade: the quality class of the produce.
  • MSP: Minimum Support Price announced by the government for some crops.
  • eNAM: the online national agriculture market.
  • Agmarknet: the government portal that publishes mandi prices.
  • Commission agent: a trader who helps arrange the sale and charges a commission.
