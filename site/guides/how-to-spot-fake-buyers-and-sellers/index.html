How to Spot Fake Buyers and Sellers: A Safety Guide

Most buyers and sellers are honest, but a few simple habits protect you from the ones who are not.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

Warning signs

  • Asks for money in advance for “booking” or “registration”.
  • Offers a price far above the market rate.
  • Pushes you to decide in a hurry.
  • Refuses a video call or a visit.

Safer habits

  • Verify the person: call, video call, and check the business location.
  • Do not pay in advance to people you have not met.
  • For large loads, agree weight, quality and payment terms in writing.
  • Prefer payment by bank transfer to cash.
  • Use marketplace Report buttons if a listing looks suspicious.

If you were cheated

Keep all messages and receipts, and report to the local police or the national cyber crime reporting portal. Tell the platform where you found the person.

Frequently asked questions

Should I pay in advance to a new buyer?

No. Avoid paying money in advance to someone you have not verified.
