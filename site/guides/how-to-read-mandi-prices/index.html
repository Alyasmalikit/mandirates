How to Read Mandi Prices: Min, Max and Modal Price

A mandi price list looks simple, but three numbers and one unit decide what it really tells you.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

The three prices

  • Minimum price: the lowest rate at which produce sold in that mandi that day.
  • Maximum price: the highest rate that day.
  • Modal price: the rate at which most of the produce sold. It is the most useful number for a quick view.

Quintal and per kg

Mandi prices are normally per quintal. One quintal is 100 kg. To get the price per kg, divide by 100. A price of ₹2,500 per quintal is ₹25 per kg.

Variety, grade and date

The same crop can sell at different prices by variety and grade. Always check the date too. A price from a few days ago is not today’s price.

When a price looks wrong

Sometimes the source data has a mistake or a different unit. If one mandi’s price is far away from the rest for the same crop, MandiRates marks it with a ⚠ sign and keeps it out of the best-price headlines. Confirm with the mandi before you act on it.

Frequently asked questions

What is modal price?

The rate at which most of the produce sold in that mandi on that day.

How many kg are in a quintal?

100 kg.
