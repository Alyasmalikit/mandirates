How to Choose the Best Mandi to Sell Your Crop

The highest price is not always the best deal. What matters is the money you keep after all costs.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

Step 1: compare prices

Use the Compare page to put up to 8 mandis side by side for your crop. Look at the modal price first.

Step 2: subtract the costs

  • Transport to the mandi
  • Loading and unloading
  • Commission and market fee
  • Waiting time and spoilage risk

The Where to sell tool subtracts transport for you and ranks mandis by what you keep.

Step 3: ask about payment

Ask how fast you will be paid and in what way. A slightly lower price with quick, safe payment can be better.

Step 4: confirm before loading

Call the mandi or a trusted trader to confirm today’s rate before you load the truck. Prices here are indicative.

Frequently asked questions

Is the highest mandi price always best?

No. Transport, commission and payment time can make a lower price better.
