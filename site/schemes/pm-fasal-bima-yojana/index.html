PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): Crop Insurance Explained

PMFBY gives insurance cover if your crop is lost to natural calamities, pests or diseases. The farmer pays only a small part of the premium.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

What does it cover?

PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana) protects farmers from crop loss caused by natural calamities, pests and diseases. If the crop is damaged, the insurance company pays a claim.

What does the farmer pay?

Under the scheme guidelines, farmers pay a fixed low share of the premium and the government pays the rest:

  • 2% for kharif food crops and oilseeds
  • 1.5% for rabi food crops and oilseeds
  • 5% for annual commercial and horticultural crops

Rates and rules can change, so confirm the current ones on pmfby.gov.in.

How to enrol

Farmers who take a crop loan are usually enrolled through the bank. Other farmers can enrol through a bank, a Common Service Centre or the official portal. Every crop and season has a last date, so enrol early.

If your crop is damaged

Tell your insurance company, bank or agriculture office as fast as you can. For some local calamities there is a short reporting window, so do not wait. Keep photos and your enrolment receipt.

Frequently asked questions

Who pays the rest of the premium?

The government pays the remaining premium under the scheme guidelines.

When should I enrol?

Before the last date for your crop and season. Check the dates on pmfby.gov.in or ask your bank.
