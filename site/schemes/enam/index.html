eNAM: India’s Online Mandi (National Agriculture Market)

eNAM links mandis across states on one online platform so farmers can sell to more buyers than the local mandi.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

What is eNAM?

eNAM (electronic National Agriculture Market) is an online trading platform that connects participating APMC mandis. Buyers anywhere on the platform can bid for your produce, which can mean better price discovery.

How does it work?

  • The farmer registers on the platform with Aadhaar and bank details.
  • The produce is brought to a mandi that is linked with eNAM.
  • It is checked for quality and graded.
  • Buyers bid online, and payment goes to the farmer’s bank account.

What to check first

Only mandis that have joined eNAM can trade on it. Ask your local mandi office whether it is linked, and read the official website for the current process.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sell from home on eNAM?

The produce usually has to reach a linked mandi for quality checks. Check the official site for the current process.
