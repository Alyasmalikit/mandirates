PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi: ₹6,000 a Year for Farmer Families

PM-KISAN gives eligible farmer families ₹6,000 every year, paid in three equal instalments of ₹2,000 straight into the bank account.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

What is PM-KISAN?

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) is a central government scheme that gives income support to eligible farmer families. It started in February 2019.

Eligible families get ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000. The money goes by Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) into the bank account.

Who can get it?

The scheme is meant for landholding farmer families. Your State or Union Territory checks and verifies each family under the scheme rules. Some categories of people are not eligible, and the official site lists them.

Registering does not by itself guarantee payment. Your details are verified first.

What you usually need

  • Aadhaar number, linked and authenticated
  • Bank account linked with Aadhaar
  • Land records
  • A working mobile number
  • e-KYC, if the portal asks for it

How to register or check your status

  • Open the official site pmkisan.gov.in only.
  • To register, use Farmers Corner, then New Farmer Registration.
  • To check payment, use Know Your Status or Beneficiary List.
  • Helpline numbers listed by the scheme: 155261 and 011-24300606.

Be careful

Nobody can “approve” your name for money. Use only the official website, the official app, your nearest agriculture office or a Common Service Centre. If you take help at a Common Service Centre, ask about any service charge first.

Frequently asked questions

How much does PM-KISAN give?

Eligible farmer families get ₹6,000 per year, paid in three instalments of ₹2,000 each.

How do I check my PM-KISAN status?

Open pmkisan.gov.in, choose Know Your Status, and enter your registration number.

Does registering guarantee the money?

No. Your details are verified by the State or Union Territory before payment.
