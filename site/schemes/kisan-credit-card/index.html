Kisan Credit Card (KCC): Cheap Farm Loans Explained

A Kisan Credit Card gives farmers short-term credit at a low interest rate, and prompt repayment can bring the effective rate down to 4%.

By MandiRates Editorial Team · Last reviewed 22 November 2026

What is the Kisan Credit Card?

The Kisan Credit Card (KCC) helps farmers get timely and affordable credit for crop expenses and related needs. It is also available for animal husbandry and fisheries.

Interest rate in simple words

Under the Modified Interest Subvention Scheme, short-term farm loans through KCC are available at 7% a year. Farmers who repay on time get an extra 3% prompt repayment incentive, which brings the effective rate to 4%.

Interest benefits apply up to a loan limit set by the government. The 2025 Union Budget announced raising the limit under this scheme from ₹3 lakh to ₹5 lakh, and the collateral-free limit has been raised to ₹2 lakh per borrower. The exact limit that applies to you depends on your bank and your repayment record, so ask your bank.

How to apply

  • Go to your bank, cooperative bank or regional rural bank.
  • Carry ID proof, address proof, land records and a photo.
  • Ask for the KCC form and the current interest and limit details in writing.

Good habits

Repay on time to keep the lower rate. Keep your receipts. Borrow only what you can repay after the harvest.

Frequently asked questions

What interest rate does KCC have?

Short-term KCC crop loans carry 7% a year, and timely repayment can bring the effective rate to 4%.

Is collateral needed?

Loans up to ₹2 lakh per borrower can be taken without collateral. Ask your bank about your case.
