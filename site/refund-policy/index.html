Refund and Cancellation Policy

How Premium payments, activation, cancellation and refunds work on MandiRates.

What you are buying

Premium is a digital service. There is nothing to ship. It switches on extra features for your MandiRates account for the period you choose.

Activation

After a successful payment, Premium is switched on for your account, usually within 24 hours. Use the same email or mobile number you use on MandiRates, so we can find your account.

If something goes wrong

If you were charged but Premium was not activated within 48 hours, write to us and we will either activate it or refund the payment. If money was debited but the payment failed, the bank or payment provider normally returns it within a few working days.

Cancellation and refunds

Premium does not renew automatically. You can stop at any time by simply not buying again. Once Premium has been activated and used, payments are not refunded, except where the law requires it.

Payments

Payments are processed securely by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.
